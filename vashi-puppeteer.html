Preschool & Daycare in Vashi Navi Mumbai

Vashi Sector 14 is an upscale and affluent neighborhood in Navi Mumbai, India. It has transformed from a small sector into one of the best and most up-market areas in the city. Vashi Sector 14 offers an array of activity centers for children of various age groups, making it a desirable place for families to live.
Vashi Sector 14 is known for its upscale residential areas, proximity to educational institutes and schools in Navi Mumbai, modern and innovative places of recreation, and major hubs for business and commerce. Family-friendly atmosphere with a variety of schools and preschools. Kidzonia International Preschool is a valuable addition to Vashi Sector 14.

Here are some highlights of Kidzonia Preschool Vashi

  • Spacious and Vibrant: Ample room for exploration, play, and socialization in a cheerful atmosphere.
  • Dedicated Play Areas: Safe and supervised area for active play, motor skill development, and fresh air.
  • Cozy Daycare and Sleeping: Comfortable napping environment with trained staff creating a soothing atmosphere.
  • Nutritious Meals: Freshly prepared, balanced meals with children’s needs in mind.
  • State-of-the-Art Classrooms: Modern teaching aids and engaging lessons to spark curiosity and love for learning.
  • Dedicated Teachers: Expert educators, mentors, and role models personalized teaching for each child’s unique needs.
