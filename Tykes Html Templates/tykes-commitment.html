Our Promise

"What We Promise Every Child,
Every Day."

Our commitment is not a marketing statement — it is built into the daily operations of every Tykes centre, from the way our classrooms are set up to the way our teachers are trained and the way parents are kept informed. Every promise below is backed by a system, a standard, and a person who is accountable for it.

❤️ To Every Child

  • ✓

    A safe, stimulating environment where they are seen as a whole person — not just a student

  • ✓

    An education that celebrates their unique intelligence and pace of development

  • ✓

    An experience that builds confidence, curiosity, and a love for learning that will last a lifetime

  • ✓

    Qualified, caring educators who treat every child with warmth, consistency, and genuine attention

🤝 To Every Family

  • ✓

    Transparent, consistent communication about your child's daily experience and developmental progress

  • ✓

    Qualified, caring educators who treat your child with the same love and attention you do

  • ✓

    A centre environment that is clean, safe, and professionally managed at all times

  • ✓

    Regular parent workshops and digital engagement platforms keeping you informed and involved every step

01
Academic Standards

Every Tykes classroom follows the same structured curriculum, lesson planning system, and child observation framework. Quality does not vary by location — it is built into the institution.

02
Continuous Improvement

Academic oversight, teacher training, curriculum updates, and parent feedback mechanisms are built into how every Tykes centre operates — not periodic reviews, but ongoing practices woven into daily operations.

03
Teacher Training

All Tykes educators undergo Kidzonia-aligned teacher training covering Tykes methodology, structured lesson planning, child observation, and parent communication practices before entering the classroom.

04
School Readiness

Children leaving Tykes Senior KG are academically, socially, and emotionally prepared for Grade 1 in any CBSE, ICSE, or IGCSE primary school — measured through structured developmental assessments.
