Why Choose Tykes

What Sets Tykes Apart

Institutional quality at every centre — not varied franchise quality, but a network of consistently excellent institutions. The same academic framework. The same operational rigour. The same child outcomes. Many preschools offer care. Many offer basic learning. Tykes Early Years offers an institution — one with defined academic standards, professional operational systems, and a curriculum backed by a decade of refinement across India's most awarded preschool network.

The Same Child. A Completely Different Outcome.

Kidzonia-grade curriculum — the same academic framework that won the #1 India ECE Excellence Index 2025 ranking. Professionally trained educators — trained in the Tykes methodology, lesson planning, and child observation practices. Every child at every Tykes centre receives the same institutional quality, regardless of location.

🏛️

Institutional Consistency

Every Tykes classroom follows identical curriculum, lesson planning systems, and child observation frameworks. Quality doesn't vary by location — it's built into the institution.

📊

Documented Child Progress

Structured assessment frameworks tracking each child's development across all domains. Parents receive detailed progress reports to understand and celebrate every milestone.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Parent Partnership

Regular communication, parent workshops, and digital engagement platforms keeping families informed and involved in their child's learning journey every step of the way.

🧩

Multiple Intelligence Approach

No child is left behind because they don't fit one type of 'smart'. We identify and celebrate every child's unique intelligence profile across linguistic, logical, spatial, and interpersonal domains.

🎓

Seamless School Readiness

Children leaving Tykes Senior KG are academically, socially, and emotionally prepared for primary school — CBSE, ICSE, or IGCSE. Our alumni enter Grade 1 with confidence and curiosity.

🔄

Continuous Improvement

Academic oversight, teacher training, curriculum updates, and parent feedback mechanisms are built into daily operations — not periodic reviews but ongoing practices woven into every centre.
