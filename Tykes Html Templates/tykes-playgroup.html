The Playgroup Journey

The First Step Into Learning
Is Always the Most Special

In our Playgroup, children step into a nurturing space where they feel safe, loved, and encouraged to explore. This stage is all about helping them adjust to a new environment while building trust with teachers and peers.

We create a warm and welcoming atmosphere where children begin to develop independence, social connections, and confidence through guided play and meaningful interactions.

Every activity, every song, every story — is thoughtfully designed to make your child's first school experience feel like a joyful extension of home.

🌸 Safe & Nurturing
💚 Caring Educators
🎨 Play-Based Learning
Programme Focus Areas

Six Pillars of the
Playgroup Experience

Every element of our Playgroup is thoughtfully crafted — building emotional comfort, social bonds, and foundational skills through the power of play.

🤗
Emotional Security

Safe, Loved & Confident

Building emotional security is our first priority. Children learn to feel safe away from home, trust their teachers, and develop the emotional resilience that underpins all future learning.

👫
Social Skills

Making Friends & Sharing Joys

Through guided group activities and free play, children learn to interact, share, take turns, and form their first friendships — the building blocks of healthy social development.

🗣️
Communication

Little Voices, Big Expressions

Storytelling, rhymes, and interactive conversations help children expand their vocabulary, express their feelings, and grow in verbal confidence in a supportive, judgment-free space.

✋
Motor Development

Moving, Creating & Discovering

Art, craft, sensory play, music, and movement activities develop both fine and gross motor skills — building the physical coordination children need for writing and beyond.

🔍
Curiosity & Exploration

Wonder-Filled Little Explorers

Sensory bins, water play, sand tables, and guided discovery activities spark natural curiosity and a love of learning that will carry children through every stage of their education.

🏡
Home-to-School Transition

A Gentle Bridge from Home

A carefully paced, personalised approach helps every child transition at their own pace — from separation anxiety support to building daily routines that feel familiar and reassuring.

5 Milestones
by Year End
Developmental Milestones

What Your Child
Will Grow Into

By the end of the Playgroup year, every Tykes child reaches these meaningful milestones — each one a sign of a happy, growing, and ready-to-flourish young learner.

1

Comfortable & Settled at School

Separates from caregivers with confidence, settles into the classroom routine, and feels genuinely safe and happy in their school environment.

2

Socially Connected & Cooperative

Engages in play with peers, shares toys and materials, participates in group activities, and forms warm bonds with teachers and classmates.

3

Communicates Needs & Feelings Clearly

Uses words to express emotions, needs, and ideas. Participates actively in rhymes, songs, and storytelling — showing growing language confidence.

4

Developing Motor Skills Through Play

Shows improved fine motor control through colouring, threading, and clay work; gross motor skills through movement, dance, and outdoor play.

✓

Ready for the Next Step — Nursery

Emotionally secure, socially engaged, and curious about the world — your Playgroup child is truly ready to take their next joyful step into Nursery.

A Typical Day

A Day in Playgroup

A warm, joyful routine where every hour brings a new adventure — and every moment builds a little child's confidence and love for school.

🌞
8:00 – 8:30 AM
Welcome Circle & Morning Song

Children arrive to a warm welcome, transition from caregivers gently, and settle into the morning circle with greetings, songs, and the joy of being together.

🎨
8:30 – 9:15 AM
Art & Creative Expression

Finger painting, stamping, tearing, and sticking — creative activities that build fine motor skills, self-expression, and the pure joy of making something with little hands.

📚
9:15 – 9:50 AM
Storytelling & Rhyme Time

Engaging picture books, action rhymes, and puppet shows that build vocabulary, listening skills, and a love for language — all delivered with warmth and fun.

🏖️
9:50 – 10:20 AM
Sensory Play (Water & Sand)

Water tables, sand pits, and sensory bins ignite curiosity, develop tactile awareness, and give children a deeply satisfying and calming exploratory experience.

🎵
10:30 – 11:00 AM
Music & Movement

Dancing, clapping rhythms, and singing build gross motor skills, coordination, listening skills, and joyful group energy — one of the most beloved parts of the Playgroup day.

🧩
11:00 – 11:30 AM
Free Play & Group Interaction

Children choose their activities freely — blocks, puzzles, role play, or drawing — while teachers gently facilitate social interactions, sharing, and early cooperative skills.

🍎
11:30 – 12:00 PM
Snack Time & Wind-Down

A calm, social snack time where children practice independence — opening lunchboxes, eating together, and chatting with friends before a gentle end-of-day circle and goodbye.

🌸
Daily
Outdoor Discovery Time

Fresh air, open space, and unstructured outdoor play — climbing, running, observing nature — build physical confidence and a joyful connection with the world around them.

Skills Development

Foundational Skills
That Last a Lifetime

At this important stage, we focus not on academics, but on the deeply human skills that every child needs to thrive — in school, in friendships, and in life.

Our trained educators build these skills through every song, story, sensory activity, and moment of guided play — patiently, lovingly, and with great intention.

💛

Emotional Expression

Children learn to name, express, and manage their emotions — a skill more valuable than any academic head start.

🤝

Social Interaction & Bonding

Making friends, sharing, and cooperating — the foundations of healthy relationships at every stage of life.

👂

Listening & Communication

Following simple instructions, listening to stories, and communicating needs clearly — building confident communicators from day one.

✋

Fine & Gross Motor Skills

Holding crayons, threading beads, running, jumping — physical development that underpins writing, sports, and daily independence.

🌱

Early Independence

Packing bags, washing hands, making choices — small acts of independence that build enormous self-confidence over time.

🔭

Curiosity & Exploration

A natural love of learning, questioning, and discovering — the most powerful gift we can give any young child.

🌸

Safe & Nurturing Environment

A warm, home-like space designed specifically for the youngest learners — where safety, comfort, and joy come first, always.

👩‍🏫

Caring & Trained Educators

Our Playgroup teachers are specially trained in early childhood development — patient, gentle, and genuinely passionate about little ones.

💚

Emotional Comfort First

We believe no child can learn until they feel emotionally safe. Every practice, every routine is designed around your child's emotional wellbeing.

🏡

Smooth Home-to-School Transition

A gradual, personalised transition process that respects every child's timeline — so they arrive excited, not anxious, every single day.

🎮

Balanced Play & Learning Routine

A beautifully balanced daily schedule of structured and free play — enough routine to feel secure, enough freedom to explore and discover.

📊

Built on 15 Years of Excellence

Backed by the academic legacy of Kidzonia International Preschools across 45+ centres — you can trust that this programme truly works.

Why Choose Tykes Playgroup

The Beginning That
Changes Everything

The Playgroup year is deceptively simple — but it is the most important year of your child's educational journey. How a child first experiences school shapes their relationship with learning for life.

At Tykes, we take this responsibility with the deepest care. Our Playgroup programme is not about worksheets or flash cards. It is about building a child who loves coming to school, trusts their teachers, and believes in themselves.

That is the gift of a great Playgroup year — and that is exactly what we deliver, every day.
