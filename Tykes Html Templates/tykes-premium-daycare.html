About Premium Daycare

More Than a Safe Space.
A Second Home.

At Tykes, our Premium Daycare is thoughtfully designed for children aged 1.5 to 6 years. It seamlessly combines full preschool curriculum with enriching afternoon activities, nutritious meals, and supervised rest — all within a warm, home-like setting.

Our flexible extended hours (7:00 am to 7:00 pm) are built around real working-parent schedules. Children can be dropped and picked up at any time within this window, with no guilt and no compromise on quality of care.

What's Included

Everything Your Child
Needs, All in One Place

Our Premium Daycare is a complete daily programme — structured, nurturing, and thoughtfully designed for every stage of your child's day.

⏰
Flexibility

Extended Hours

Open from 7:00 am to 7:00 pm, flexible to centre timings. Children can be dropped and picked up within this window, accommodating varied work schedules without stress.

📚
Learning

Structured Learning Programme

Full preschool curriculum (Nursery / JKG / SKG as appropriate) during morning hours, followed by afternoon enrichment activities, creative sessions, and free play.

🍱
Nutrition

Nutrition & Meals

Morning snack, nutritious hot lunch (menu shared weekly with parents), and evening snack. Dietary requirements and allergies are accommodated. Supervised meal preparation.

😴
Rest & Wellbeing

Rest & Sleep Facilities

Dedicated nap area with individual mats/cots, fresh linen, and supervised rest time aligned to each child's natural rhythm — because rest is as important as play.

🛡️
Safety

Safety & Supervision

CCTV monitoring throughout the day, trained caregivers, strict pickup authorisation protocols, regular health and hygiene checks, and first aid trained staff on-site at all times.

📲
Parent Connect

Daily Parent Updates

Daily digital updates via parent app — photos, activity summaries, meal records, nap times, and health notes. Parents stay connected without disrupting the child's experience.

Nutrition & Rest Programme

Every aspect of our nutrition and rest programme is designed with child development at its core — because a well-fed, well-rested child is a happy, ready-to-learn child.

🍎

Morning Snack

A wholesome morning snack to fuel the learning hours, carefully selected to provide sustained energy throughout the morning curriculum.

Nutritious Hot Lunch

Daily Schedule

A Day in Premium Daycare

🌅
7:00 – 9:00 AM
Warm Welcome & Morning Snack

Children arrive, settle in, and enjoy a nutritious morning snack in a calm, welcoming atmosphere.

📚
9:00 – 12:00 PM
Structured Learning

Full preschool curriculum — Nursery, JKG, or SKG as appropriate — with guided activities and circle time.

🍛
12:00 – 1:00 PM
Hot Lunch & Hygiene

A nutritious hot lunch followed by hand-washing and oral hygiene routines — building lifelong healthy habits.

😴

Rest & Nap Time

Supervised rest in a quiet, dedicated nap area with individual mats, fresh linen, and soft music.

🎨
2:30 – 4:30 PM
Enrichment & Creative Sessions

Art, music, storytelling, sensory play, and hands-on enrichment activities designed to spark curiosity and creativity.

🥪
4:30 – 5:00 PM
Evening Snack

A light, wholesome snack to recharge — and time to chat with friends and educators.

🌳
5:00 – 6:30 PM
Free Play & Outdoor Time

Child-led free play, outdoor activity, and movement to wind down the day in a joyful, unstructured way.

🌙
6:30 – 7:00 PM
Wind-Down & Pickup

Calm wind-down with quiet activities. Authorised pickup with daily digital update sent to parents via the app.
