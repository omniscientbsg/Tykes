Curriculum Philosophy

We Don't Separate
Play from Learning.

Brain science, developmental psychology, and decades of classroom evidence confirm that young children learn most deeply when they are engaged, curious, and emotionally safe. Our curriculum creates the conditions for all three.

The Tykes curriculum is thematic, experiential, and deeply child-centred. It draws on the world's most respected early childhood education philosophies and translates them into a structured, consistent daily experience that every child at every Tykes centre receives.

How We Teach

Learning Methodologies
at a Glance

Many preschools offer care. Many offer basic learning. Tykes Early Years offers an institution — one with defined academic standards, professional operational systems, and a curriculum backed by a decade of refinement across India's most awarded preschool network.

🗓️

Thematic Learning

Monthly themes connect all subject areas into a coherent, meaningful experience. Children explore topics through classroom projects, experiments, field trips, role-play, and collaborative activities. Children see how knowledge connects — not how it is divided into subjects.

Monthly Projects Cross-Curricular Collaborative
🔬

Experiential Learning

Learning through doing — science experiments, cooking sessions, field trips, planting, and construction. Concepts are experienced before they are labelled. Children build genuine understanding through direct engagement with the physical world.

Hands-On Science Field Trips Real-World Context
🎮

Play-Based Learning

Structured free play, guided play, and collaborative play timetabled throughout the day — the primary vehicle through which social-emotional, cognitive, and physical skills develop simultaneously. Play is not a break. Play is the work of childhood.

Free Play Guided Exploration Social-Emotional
💻

Digital Learning Integration

Smart boards, structured educational apps, and digital storytelling tools woven into daily practice — enhancing, not replacing, human learning interactions. Children become comfortable with technology while never losing the primacy of teacher-child connection.

Daily Schedule

"Every Hour Has a Purpose.
Every Moment Has a Memory."

A typical day at a Tykes Early Years centre is carefully structured to balance learning, play, rest, and social interaction — honouring the child's developmental needs while building the skills and habits that will serve them throughout their education.

7:30 – 8:30 am

🌅 Morning Arrival & Free Play

Children arrive and settle with open access to learning corners. Teachers observe and facilitate child-initiated play. Builds emotional security and independence through a consistent transitional routine.

8:30 – 9:00 am

🌞 Morning Circle Time

Whole-class gathering for greetings, calendar and weather activities, theme introduction, and music and movement. Builds community, language, and the habit of focused group participation.

9:00 – 9:45 am

📖 Structured Learning Activity

Teacher-facilitated activity aligned to the monthly theme — language, numeracy, science, or arts. Designed to introduce or consolidate a specific concept using hands-on materials.

9:45 – 10:15 am

🍎 Snack & Social Time

Healthy snack break with structured table manners practice, peer conversation, and independent tasks. Life skills and social grace in a real, everyday context.

10:15 – 11:00 am

🔄 Learning Corner Rotations

Children rotate through structured learning corners in small groups. Teachers facilitate, observe, and document learning as it happens.

📚 Library 🔬 Discovery 🎨 Art Corner 🧱 Construction 🎭 Dramatic Play 💻 Digital
11:00 – 11:30 am

🌳 Outdoor Play & Physical Activity

Structured outdoor session combining gross motor skill development, free movement, cooperative games, and nature exploration. Physical development is a curriculum priority, not an add-on.

11:30 am – 12:00 pm

📖 Story Time, Art & Closing Circle

Guided story session, art or craft aligned to theme, and closing reflection where children share what they discovered. Builds language, listening, and metacognitive habits.

12:00 – 6:00 pm · DayCare Children

🏡 DayCare Extended Hours

For full-day DayCare children: hot lunch, afternoon rest, enrichment activities, and pickup anytime until 6 pm. Full supervision by trained staff throughout. The day continues — enriched, not just extended.
