About the Programme

Corporate DayCare Is Not a Benefit.
It Is a Business Investment.

Tykes Early Years Corporate DayCare is a structured, professionally managed childcare solution designed for corporate organisations, business parks, IT campuses, hospitals, and large institutions whose employees need reliable, high-quality childcare close to or at their workplace.

When a parent knows their child is in expert hands — with a curriculum-led programme, nutritious meals, and real-time digital updates — they bring their full self to work. That is measurable value for your organisation.

Corporate DayCare is not just a perk on a benefits sheet. It is a strategic retention tool, a productivity lever, and a statement about the kind of organisation you are.

The Business Case

Why Forward-Thinking
Organisations Choose Corporate DayCare

The ROI is clear. The impact is measurable. The statement it makes about your culture is lasting.

📉 ↓ Attrition

Reduces Employee Attrition

Working parents — especially mothers returning from maternity leave — are significantly more likely to stay with organisations that provide or subsidise childcare. Retain the talent you have invested years in developing.

🎯 ↑ Focus

Increases Productivity

Childcare anxiety is a leading cause of workplace distraction and absenteeism. When parents trust their childcare arrangement completely, productivity, attendance, and engagement measurably improve — from day one.

🏆 ↑ Brand

Strengthens Employer Brand

In competitive talent markets, corporate childcare is a powerful differentiator. It signals that your organisation values the whole person, not just the professional — and that signal reaches candidates before they even apply.

⚖️ ↑ Equity

Supports Gender Equity

Childcare is disproportionately a women's burden. Corporate DayCare directly addresses the childcare barrier that causes talented women to exit the workforce — making it simultaneously an equity issue and a talent issue, solved in one investment.

Partnership Models

Three Models. One Right Fit
for Your Organisation.

Whether you are a large campus, a mid-size office, or a distributed multi-location organisation — we have a Corporate DayCare model designed for exactly your context.

On-Campus Centre
🏢
On-Campus Centre

A dedicated Tykes centre, built within or adjacent to your campus — fully managed by us.

A full Tykes Early Years DayCare centre is set up and operated within or adjacent to the corporate campus. All staffing, curriculum, operations, meals, and parent communication are managed entirely by Tykes — giving your HR team zero operational burden.

Best For → Large corporates with 500+ employees, IT parks, hospital campuses, and SEZ environments.
Nearby Centre Partnership
📍
Nearby Centre Partnership

Reserved seats at the nearest Tykes centre — corporate billing, dedicated support.

Reserved seats and preferential enrolment at the nearest Tykes Early Years centre for your employees — with dedicated corporate billing, custom reporting, and a dedicated communication channel for your HR team. High-quality childcare, zero setup.

Best For → Mid-size organisations, offices in residential catchments, and growing companies.
Subsidy Programme
💳
Corporate Subsidy Programme

Employees enrol at any Tykes centre; you cover a structured contribution.

Employees enrol at any Tykes Early Years centre across our network, and the corporate contribution is managed through a structured billing arrangement directly with Tykes. Maximum flexibility for both the organisation and its employees.

Best For → Multi-location organisations with distributed employee bases and remote-work policies.

How We Work Together

The Corporate Partnership Process

From first conversation to fully operational centre — our proven four-step process makes setting up Corporate DayCare straightforward, structured, and stress-free for your team.

1
Step One
Initial Consultation

A Tykes team member meets with your HR/Admin leadership to understand employee demographics, childcare demand, location constraints, and programme preferences — with zero obligation.

2
Step Two
Needs Assessment & Proposal

We prepare a customised proposal detailing the recommended model, capacity, pricing structure, and service inclusions — tailored precisely to your organisation's size, culture, and location.

3
Step Three
Agreement & Setup

Corporate agreement signed. Centre setup, staffing, and infrastructure configured to specification. Employee communication, enrolment process, and parent onboarding all managed by Tykes.

4
Step Four
Launch & Ongoing Management

Centre goes operational with a dedicated account manager in place. Monthly HR reporting, ongoing quality oversight, and periodic review meetings keep the partnership performing at its best.
