5 Simple Steps

The Admissions Process

A clear, welcoming, and friction-less admissions journey for families — from first enquiry to welcome orientation in 5 guided steps.

📝
Step 01
Submit an Enquiry
Fill the online form or call your nearest Tykes centre. Acknowledgement within 24 hours, call from admissions team within 48 hours.
🏫
Step 02
Book a Centre Visit
We warmly invite you and your child for a guided tour. Meet the teachers, explore learning spaces, and feel the Tykes environment in person.
🎯
Step 03
Programme Consultation
Speak with our academic team about the right programme for your child's age and developmental stage. We fit programmes to children — not the other way.
✅
Step 04
Complete Enrolment
Submit the registration form and required documents. Pay the registration fee to confirm your child's seat. Your advisor guides every step.
🎉
Step 05
Welcome Orientation
Before Day 1, you and your child attend our Welcome Orientation — so the classroom, teachers, and routine are already familiar on the first day.

What to Bring

Documents Required

These documents are required when completing enrolment at Step 4. You don't need anything for an initial enquiry or centre visit — just come and meet us.

01
Child's Birth Certificate

Original for verification; photocopy retained for centre records

02
Child's Passport-Size Photographs

4 recent passport-sized photographs of the child

03
Parent / Guardian ID Proof

Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Passport (any one)

04
Parent / Guardian Address Proof

Aadhaar, utility bill, or any government-issued address document

05
Child's Immunisation Record

Vaccination card covering age-appropriate immunisations

06
Medical Fitness Certificate

Required for DayCare enrolments — issued by a registered physician

07
Emergency Contact Details

Minimum 2 emergency contacts with name, relationship, and mobile number

08
Medical / Developmental Information

Optional but encouraged — helps us customise your child's programme from Day 1

💡 Don't worry about documents at the enquiry stage. Your admissions advisor will share a complete checklist after your centre visit and guide you through the submission process. We make it easy.
