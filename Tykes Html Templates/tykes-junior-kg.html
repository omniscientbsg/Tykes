About the Programme

Where Every Question
Becomes a Discovery

Junior KG at Tykes is designed for children who are naturally brimming with "why?" — and our educators are trained to honour every single one. This is the age of structured curiosity, where questions are not just welcome, they are the engine of our entire programme.

Through thematic project work, guided experimentation, collaborative problem-solving, and literacy and numeracy foundations rooted in play, children build the academic readiness and emotional confidence they need for a lifetime of learning.

Every day in Junior KG is purposefully designed — with a balance of structured learning, creative exploration, physical activity, and social development — so that children leave not just school-ready, but life-ready.

Programme Focus Areas

Six Pillars of the
Junior KG Experience

Every area of our curriculum is intentionally woven together — academic foundations meet creative expression, and individual discovery meets collaborative growth.

📖
Literacy Development

Reading Readiness & Early Writing

Phonics-based reading readiness, sight word introduction, early writing practice including letter formation, and comprehension through storytelling and drama — building confident, expressive communicators.

🔢
Numeracy & Early Maths

Maths Through Play & Real Life

Counting to 20+, number recognition, basic addition through manipulatives, patterns, measurement, and sorting by attributes — mathematics embedded in real-world contexts that make sense to children.

🏗️
Thematic Project Work

Monthly Themes, Deep Learning

Monthly themes explored through classroom projects — research at a child's level, building, creating, presenting. Develops sustained attention, inquiry skills, and genuine pride in achievement.

🧪
Science & Discovery

Little Scientists, Big Questions

Simple experiments, nature study, cause-and-effect exploration. Children are scientists — we give them the tools and language to observe, question, predict, and conclude with confidence.

🤝
Social Skills & Emotional Intelligence

Empathy, Leadership & Resilience

Conflict resolution, empathy development, leadership opportunities, and structured cooperative play — building emotional intelligence foundational to all future relationships and community participation.

🎨
Arts, Music & Movement

Expression Through Every Medium

Weekly music sessions, movement and dance, drama, and visual arts — developing aesthetic sensibility, self-expression, and bodily intelligence alongside core academic skills.

6 Key Milestones
by Year End
Developmental Milestones

What Your Child
Will Achieve

By the end of Junior KG at Tykes, children reach these evidence-based milestones — each one a foundation for confident, joyful learning in Senior KG and beyond.

1

Phonics-Ready Reader

Reads simple 3-letter CVC words and recognises all letter sounds — fully prepared for structured phonics progression.

2

Confident Writer

Writes letters of the alphabet and simple words with increasing control and growing pride in their own written expression.

3

Early Mathematician

Counts to 30+, understands number before and after, and can do simple sums with manipulatives — making maths tangible and meaningful.

4

Articulate Communicator

Participates confidently in group discussions and sustains story-related conversations, listening and responding with growing sophistication.

5

Independent Project Thinker

Completes a project task independently over multiple sessions — demonstrating sustained attention, planning, and a genuine sense of accomplishment.

6

Emotionally Intelligent Peer

Demonstrates empathy and uses words to resolve peer conflicts — a social-emotional milestone that underpins every relationship in school and life.

A Typical Day

A Day in Junior KG

Every hour is purposeful. Every moment is joyful. Here's how a day unfolds for our Curious Builders.

🌅
8:00 – 8:30 AM
Morning Circle & Mindfulness

Children gather for morning greetings, calendar time, and a short mindfulness moment — setting intentions and building community.

🔤
8:30 – 9:15 AM
Phonics & Literacy Block

Structured phonics sessions, sight word practice, and guided reading — building the language foundation children need for independent reading.

🔢
9:15 – 10:00 AM
Numeracy & Maths Play

Hands-on maths with manipulatives, counting games, and real-world problem solving — making numbers come alive through play and discovery.

🏗️
10:00 – 10:45 AM
Thematic Project Time

Children dive into the month's theme — building, creating, researching, and presenting. The heart of the Junior KG Curious Builders experience.

🍎
10:45 – 11:15 AM
Snack & Outdoor Play

Nutritious snack time followed by supervised outdoor play — developing gross motor skills, social bonds, and a love of physical activity.

🧪
11:15 AM – 12:00 PM
Science Discovery or Arts

Alternating between science experiments and arts, music, and movement — nurturing both the analytical and creative minds within every child.

📚
12:00 – 12:30 PM
Story Time & Reflection

A shared story, group discussion, and reflection on the day — building comprehension, vocabulary, and the habit of thoughtful conversation.

👋
12:30 PM
Farewell Circle

Children share one thing they discovered or felt proud of — closing the day with recognition, gratitude, and excitement for tomorrow.
