Establishing a preschool independently requires years of curriculum development, operational system building, brand creation, and market credibility. The Tykes Early Years franchise model eliminates this journey — giving you immediate access to everything it took Kidzonia a decade to build.

The Opportunity

Most independent preschool founders spend 3–5 years just building the fundamentals: a curriculum, a brand identity, operational protocols, and community trust. We hand you all of it on day one.

The Tykes franchise isn't a licence. It's a complete institutional transfer — the same systems, frameworks, and credibility that power Kidzonia's 45+ COCO centres, packaged for your territory.

Early childhood education is one of India's most recession-resilient sectors. Urban families consistently prioritise structured, professional preschool education — creating stable, long-term demand for quality operators.
