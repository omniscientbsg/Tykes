Complete Support System

Three Phases. Total Coverage.

Before you open your doors, we make sure everything behind them is built to Tykes standards — from the ambience of your classrooms to the readiness of your team.

🏛️

Infrastructure & Ambience Planning

Layout guidance aligned to Tykes brand standards and safety requirements — so every corner of your centre reflects the quality parents expect.

🪑

Equipment & Material Sourcing

Guidance on furniture, classroom equipment, and learning material procurement — approved suppliers, specifications, and setup standards included.

📚

Curriculum Orientation

Full training on lesson planning, learning outcomes, and classroom management — so your academic delivery is aligned to the award-winning Tykes framework from Day 1.

👩‍🏫

Hiring Framework

Job descriptions, interview guidance, and teacher training preparation — so you recruit educators who are aligned to your centre's culture and capable of delivering from the start.

📋

Regulatory Guidance

Documentation and compliance support for operating a preschool in your state — we help you navigate the regulatory landscape so you open with full legal clarity.

🤝

Franchise Onboarding

A structured induction into the Tykes ecosystem — brand values, operational standards, and the systems every franchise partner must know before Day 1.

Launch month is the most critical window for any new centre. We deploy dedicated support across marketing, branding, admissions, and operations to make your opening month count.

🌐

Marketing & Online Presence Activation

Google Business setup, social media activation, and local digital presence — your centre goes live online the moment your doors open.

🎨

Preschool Branding Implementation

Signage, classroom branding, uniforms, and parent communication materials — every touchpoint your community sees reflects the Tykes brand standard.

📣

Admissions Campaign Support

Digital creatives, enquiry management scripts, and conversion guidance — structured tools to turn interest into enrolments during the critical launch window.

🏫

Classroom Readiness Execution

Supervised setup of all learning zones, material placement, and display boards — your classrooms are audit-ready before the first child walks in.

🎉

Inauguration Support

Launch event planning and community activation guidance — create a memorable opening day that introduces your centre to the neighbourhood and builds early trust.

📞

Parent Communication Toolkit

Ready-to-use communication templates, fee collection scripts, and parent onboarding guides — so every family interaction from day one reflects the Tykes standard.

Our support doesn't end at launch. Ongoing guidance across academics, operations, marketing, and growth keeps your centre on a continuous improvement trajectory.

📖

Ongoing Academic Guidance

Monthly curriculum updates, lesson plan support, and child observation frameworks — so your academic delivery stays fresh, relevant, and aligned to outcomes.

⚙️

Operational Support

Periodic reviews, compliance checks, and process improvement guidance — keeping your centre running at the standard parents chose you for.

📊

Marketing Consultation

Ongoing digital strategy, seasonal campaign support, and content guidance — so your centre stays visible and continues driving enquiries beyond launch month.

📈

Growth Advisory

When you're ready to open your second centre, we're already building the pathway. Franchisees who perform have a clear, supported route to multi-centre expansion.

⭐

Annual Centre Review

A comprehensive yearly performance and quality audit — covering academics, operations, parent satisfaction, and brand standards. Your roadmap for the year ahead, built together.

👥

Franchisee Community

Access to an active network of Tykes and Kidzonia franchise partners — shared learnings, seasonal best practices, and a peer group that understands the journey you're on.

🤝

Continuous Partnership — Not a One-Time Handover

Most franchise models give you a manual and step back. The Tykes model is built on ongoing engagement — because your success is how we build the brand, one centre at a time.

Strategic Consultation

20–45
