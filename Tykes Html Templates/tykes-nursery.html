Programme Overview

A Second Home Where
Wonder Begins

Nursery at Tykes Early Years is where a child's first formal learning journey begins. Our programme is built around the understanding that children aged 2.5 to 3.5 years need warmth first, structure second — and always, the freedom to explore at their own pace.

Our teachers are trained to make separation from caregivers gentle, consistent, and positive — creating a second home where children quickly feel safe enough to play, connect, and discover. Every corner, every routine, every interaction is intentional.

What We Focus On

Six Pillars of the Nursery Programme

Six key developmental areas woven into every day — building the foundations of a lifetime of learning.

🗣️
Language

Language & Communication

Action songs, picture books, storytelling, puppets, and rhymes build vocabulary, listening skills, and the joy of spoken language — in a pressure-free, playful environment.

🤲
Motor Skills

Sensory & Motor Development

Water play, sand play, clay, playdough, threading, pouring, and stacking develop fine and gross motor skills simultaneously — because children learn through their hands.

🤝
Social-Emotional

Social & Emotional Learning

Learning to share, take turns, express feelings, and develop trust in peers and teachers — the social-emotional foundation upon which all future learning is built.

🎨
Creativity

Creative Expression

Open-ended art, music, movement, and dramatic play allow children to express and process their inner world safely, joyfully, and without judgement.

🔢
Early Academics

Early Numeracy & Literacy

Number concepts through counting games and sorting. Pre-literacy through alphabet exposure, print awareness, and phonological play — introduced playfully, never pressured.

🌳
Physical

Outdoor & Physical Play

Running, climbing, balancing, and cooperative games develop physical confidence, coordination, and the ability to navigate social play with peers in the real world.

Daily Structure

Every Moment Has a Purpose

7:30 – 8:30 amMorning Arrival

Morning Arrival & Free Play

Children arrive and settle with open access to learning corners. Teachers observe and facilitate child-initiated play, building emotional security and independence through a consistent routine.

8:30 – 9:00 amCircle Time

Morning Circle Time

Whole-class gathering for greetings, calendar and weather activities, theme introduction, and music and movement. Builds community, language, and the habit of focused group participation.

9:00 – 9:45 amCore Learning

Structured Learning Activity

Teacher-facilitated activity aligned to the monthly theme — language, numeracy, science, or arts — designed to introduce or consolidate a specific concept using hands-on materials.

9:45 – 10:15 amSocial Time

Snack & Social Time

Healthy snack break with structured table manners practice, peer conversation, and independent tasks. Life skills and social grace developed in a real, everyday context.

10:15 – 11:00 amExploration

Learning Corner Rotations

Children rotate through structured learning corners — Library, Discovery, Art, Construction, Dramatic Play, and Digital — in small groups. Teachers facilitate, observe, and document learning.

11:00 – 11:30 amPhysical

Outdoor Play & Physical Activity

Structured outdoor session combining gross motor skill development, free movement, cooperative games, and nature exploration. Physical development is a curriculum priority, not an add-on.

11:30 am – 12:00 pmReflection

Story Time, Art & Closing Circle

Guided story session, art or craft aligned to theme, and closing reflection where children share what they discovered. Builds language, listening, and metacognitive habits.

12:00 – 7:00 pmExtended Care

Premium Daycare Extension

For full-day Daycare children: hot lunch, supervised rest, afternoon enrichment activities, evening snack, and flexible pickup until 7 pm. Full supervision by trained staff throughout.

6 Key Milestones
By Year End

Developmental Milestones

What every child at the Nursery level achieves by the end of the year — observed, documented, and celebrated by our educators.

✓

Comfortable separating from caregiver and participating in the classroom independently

✓

Recognises own name in print; can write name with support from teacher

✓

Counts to 10 and understands basic quantity concepts (more / less)

✓

Uses sentences of 4–6 words to communicate needs and observations with peers and teachers

✓

Participates in group activities and demonstrates basic sharing and turn-taking behaviour

✓

Demonstrates age-appropriate control of pencil, scissors, and basic art tools
