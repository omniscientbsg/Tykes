Who We Are

Building Institutions
That Shape Foundations

Tykes Early Years is a modern early childhood institution rooted in the academic philosophy of Kidzonia International Preschools — one of India's most recognised preschool networks with 45+ centres and 18,000+ children nurtured.

Our approach combines structured curriculum delivery, disciplined operational systems, and thoughtfully designed learning environments. Every Tykes centre operates with consistency, professional management, and institutional clarity — giving children the structured start they deserve and parents the confidence they need.

With 18,000+ children nurtured, 45+ profitable COCO centres across Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, and beyond, and 50+ awards and recognitions, Kidzonia International Preschools has established a benchmark for early childhood education in India. Tykes Early Years carries this legacy forward — sharper in focus, stronger in structure, and designed to scale.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Every institution needs a north star. Ours is defined by two clear commitments — to children and to the network we are building.

🔭

Our Vision

To establish preschool institutions that foster confidence, independence, and strong early learning foundations — giving every child a structured, joyful, and professionally guided start to their educational journey.

🚀

Our Mission

To build a network of academically consistent, operationally superior, and profitable preschool centres through strong franchise partnerships and continuous institutional support — expanding access to quality early childhood education across India.
