About the Programme

Where Every Child Becomes School-Ready and Life-Ready

Senior KG is where everything comes together. This is the year a child consolidates all the skills, habits, and knowledge built over their Tykes journey — and prepares with genuine readiness and confidence for formal primary schooling.

By the end of Senior KG, a Tykes child does not merely know how to read, write, and count. They know how to think, question, collaborate, persist through challenges, and communicate their ideas clearly.

That is the Senior KG promise — not just academic preparation, but whole-child readiness for everything that comes next.

Programme Focus Areas

Six Pillars of the
Senior KG Experience

Advanced academics meet leadership development, critical thinking, and school readiness — every element designed to send children into Grade 1 with full confidence.

📚
Advanced Literacy

Fluent Readers & Creative Writers

Reading fluency, comprehension, creative writing including sentences and short paragraphs, and public presentation skills. Children read aloud, discuss books, and compose their own stories.

🔢
Advanced Numeracy

Grade 1 Maths Readiness

Addition and subtraction with and without carrying, place value to 100, basic multiplication concepts, word problems, and mathematical reasoning — preparing children for Grade 1 curriculum demands.

🧠
Critical Thinking & Problem Solving

Thinkers, Debaters & Builders

Structured thinking activities, logic puzzles, STEM challenges, and classroom debates — developing the analytical skills primary school increasingly demands from Day 1.

🏆
Leadership & Responsibility

Real Leaders in the Making

Class monitors, project leaders, school event participation, and responsibility tasks — giving children real leadership experience within a structured, supportive environment.

🌍
Environmental & Social Awareness

Thoughtful Community Members

Community helpers, environmental education, cultural awareness, and values education — developing children as thoughtful, aware members of their community and the wider world.

📋
School Readiness Assessment

Structured, Transparent Progress

Structured assessment across all developmental domains. A detailed progress report is shared with parents to support primary school placement and transition planning with full confidence.

6 Milestones
by Year End
Developmental Milestones

What Your Child
Will Achieve

By the end of Senior KG, every Tykes child reaches these evidence-based milestones — each one a clear signal of complete readiness for Grade 1 and all the rich learning that follows.

1

Fluent, Comprehending Reader

Reads grade-appropriate books with fluency and comprehension — moving beyond decoding to genuine understanding and enjoyment of text.

2

Independent Creative Writer

Writes 3–5 sentence stories or descriptions independently — structuring ideas, using punctuation, and expressing a personal voice on the page.

3

Advanced Early Mathematician

Performs addition and subtraction to 100 and understands place value — a solid mathematical foundation that meets or exceeds Grade 1 entry expectations.

4

Confident Public Presenter

Presents confidently to a group through show-and-tell and project presentations — developing poise, clarity, and genuine pride in sharing ideas.

5

Self-Regulated, Persistent Learner

Demonstrates self-regulation, persistence, and the ability to work independently for 20+ minutes — the single most important readiness skill for formal schooling.

✓

Fully Grade 1 Ready

Ready for Grade 1 in any CBSE, ICSE, or IGCSE primary school — academically, socially, emotionally, and behaviourally prepared for the next chapter.

A Typical Day

A Day in Senior KG

Purposeful structure. Joyful learning. Every hour in Senior KG builds the future leader your child is becoming.

🌅
8:00 – 8:30 AM
Leadership Circle & Calendar

Class monitor leads the morning circle. Calendar time, news sharing, and the day's agenda — building ownership, communication, and early leadership habits.

📖
8:30 – 9:20 AM
Advanced Literacy Block

Fluency reading, guided comprehension, creative writing, and story composition — structured sessions that develop true reading and writing independence.

🔢
9:20 – 10:10 AM
Advanced Numeracy & Problem Solving

Place value, addition and subtraction to 100, word problems, and mathematical reasoning — maths sessions designed to meet and exceed Grade 1 curriculum entry.

🧠
10:10 – 10:50 AM
Critical Thinking & STEM

Logic puzzles, STEM challenges, group debates, and structured inquiry — developing the analytical and collaborative thinking skills primary school demands from Day 1.

🍎
10:50 – 11:20 AM
Snack & Outdoor Play

Nutritious break and supervised outdoor play — peer negotiation, team games, and physical development all happening naturally alongside rest and social bonding.

🌍
11:20 AM – 12:00 PM
Social & Environmental Studies

Community helpers, world around us, cultural awareness, and values education — building the awareness and empathy of a thoughtful, engaged global citizen.

🎨
12:00 – 12:30 PM
Arts, Music or Drama

Rotating creative sessions — visual art, music, or drama performance — nurturing self-expression and aesthetic intelligence alongside the academic programme.

🎤
12:30 PM
Presentation & Reflection Circle

Children present work, share what they learned, and reflect on the day — building public speaking confidence, metacognitive awareness, and pride in their achievements.
